# Subscription Renewal: Evaluating Potential Proxy Metrics

[Русский](README.md) | **English** · [Portfolio](../README.en.md)

## Business Question

Which early indicators of user behavior could help assess future subscription renewal before the renewal event is observed?

## Data

Synthetic subscription customer data containing numerical and categorical variables related to user activity and additional services.

## Approach

- Defined criteria for evaluating candidate proxy metrics.
- Examined relationships between candidate metrics and subscription renewal.
- Applied Pearson correlation, Cramér's V, and point-biserial correlation depending on variable types.
- Assessed whether statistically associated metrics met the requirements of a useful early indicator.

## Key Findings

- Additional-service usage emerged as a promising candidate for further investigation.
- Statistical association alone is insufficient to establish a reliable proxy metric.
- Further validation would be needed to assess stability, predictive usefulness, and suitability for experimentation.
